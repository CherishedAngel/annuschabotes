What Remains of Me

She survived everything… except herself.

Chapter Preview

We loved her to death

Some wounds never heal. Some people never truly survive what happened to them. What Remains Of Me is a raw and emotionally gripping story about pain, love, loss, and the fragile pieces left behind after life tears someone apart. In a world filled with betrayal, emotional scars, and memories that refuse to die, healing becomes more terrifying than suffering itself. Because sometimes the hardest thing isn’t surviving. It’s learning how to live afterward.

Chapter Preview

We loved her to death

The rain had teeth. Rain sliced through flesh like frozen razors, each drop hammering Angel's coffin like bullets counting down to hell. The sound was all wrong—too hollow, too empty. Like the cavity between her ribs where her heart used to slam against bone, the silence that followed her last, choking gasp. Draven stayed back from the mourners, jaw clenched so tight his mouth flooded with copper. Even through the downpour, he could smell them—those lilies the Company sent. Manufactured blooms that had never tasted real earth or honest sunlight, perfect as everything else those bastards touched. Perfect as they'd tried to make her.
