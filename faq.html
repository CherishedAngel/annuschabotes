Questions From the Shadows

Frequently Whispered Questions

The Stories

What kind of stories do you write?

I write dark fantasy romance filled with shadow magic, emotionally intense characters, dangerous relationships, morally grey forces, and slow-burning tension.

Are your stories connected?

Some stories exist within separate worlds, while others may share hidden connections, recurring themes, or larger mythologies woven throughout the universe.

Are the books themselves written by AI?

No. The stories, characters, worldbuilding, and writing are created by Annuscha Botes.

How can I contact the author?

You can reach out through the contact page or official social media links listed on the website.

Purchases & Downloads

How do I purchase a digital copy?

Books can be purchased directly through this website using secure payment providers such as Payhip.

How will I receive my book after purchase?

After payment is completed, you will automatically receive a download link for your digital book when fulfillment is connected through the secure provider. Manual delivery may be used during setup.

Can I share my PDF with others?

Please do not redistribute, upload, or share purchased files. Supporting indie authors helps keep future stories alive.

Do you offer refunds?

Due to the nature of digital products, refunds are generally not available once files have been downloaded. If you experience technical issues, feel free to contact me.

What formats are available?

Available formats may include PDF, EPUB, audiobook editions, or preview samples depending on the title and release stage.

Can I read a sample before purchasing?

Yes. Most books include a free sample chapter or preview so readers can explore the story before deciding to purchase the full version.

Are there any physical editions available?

Right now only paperback is available on Amazon. More physical editions may become available in the future depending on demand, printing options, and upcoming releases.

Content & Warnings

Are your books intended for mature readers?

Yes. My books are written for mature readers and may include fantasy violence, trauma, explicit themes, emotional abuse, and darker relationship dynamics. Please review the Trigger Warnings page before reading.

Do your books contain explicit or disturbing content?

Some books may include mature romance, violence, captivity, grief, psychological tension, or emotionally dark scenes. Content warnings are provided whenever possible to help readers make informed choices.

Privacy & Accounts

Will my personal information be used for ads, spam, or malicious purposes?

Your personal information is never intentionally sold or misused. Information may only be shared with essential service providers required for payments, website functionality, email delivery, or order fulfillment.

Can I subscribe for updates or future releases?

Yes. You may subscribe to receive updates about new releases, projects, artwork, and upcoming stories.

Artwork & Creative Process

Who owns the rights to the books and artwork?

All stories, characters, writing, worldbuilding, branding, and original creative content belong to Annuscha Botes unless otherwise stated. Unauthorized redistribution, reproduction, or commercial use is prohibited.

Are the artworks or visuals AI-generated?

Some promotional visuals or artistic assets may use AI-assisted tools as part of the creative process unless otherwise stated.
