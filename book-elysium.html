Eclipse of Elysium

Where shadow first kissed the light.

Chapter Preview

The Crimson Pact

The Order commands her to wield the light. But the shadows whisper her true name. Elysia Nightshade was raised to obey the Golden Age Order, bound to the brilliance of their sacred light. Yet inside her stirs a forbidden power: shadow magic, hungering to be unleashed. When her path collides with Cain Brisha, a beastman cloaked in darkness, the shadows within her surge toward him, awakening desires as perilous as prophecy itself.

As Elysium trembles on the edge of eclipse, Elysia must choose between the power she fears, the love that tempts her, and the destiny that could consume her.

Chapter Preview

The Crimson Pact

Blood dripped from Elysia Nightshade's fingertips as she pushed open the weathered door of the Twisted Thorn tavern. Not her blood. A reminder of those who'd tried to prevent her from reaching this rendezvous. She flexed her hand, azure energy briefly illuminating the crimson stains before she wiped them clean on her cloak. The vision that had awakened her three nights ago still burned behind her eyes: a realm unbalanced, drowning in false light while, necessary darkness, was systematically extinguished."Balance requires sacrifice," she whispered to herself as patrons instinctively cleared a path before her. The Twisted Thorn enveloped her in a maelstrom of sensations. Ancient smoke-stained rafters creaked overhead like the ribcage of some primordial beast, supporting a ceiling blackened by decades of hearth fires.
