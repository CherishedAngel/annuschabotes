Author

Annuscha Botes

Annuscha, also known online as CherishedAngel, is a South African fantasy author, artist, and creative storyteller whose imagination has been alive since childhood. Although she spent years building worlds and characters in notebooks and scattered files, she only began pursuing writing seriously after encouraging her daughters to chase their own dreams. In inspiring them, she reignited her own.

Today her stories are filled with dark magic, emotional chaos, dangerous love, and worlds balanced on the edge of destruction. Her hard drive has become a growing archive of unfinished novels, unexpected plot twists, and characters who refuse to stay quiet.

Certain themes echo through all her stories: blue carries power, magic is never far away, war lingers on the horizon, and love is almost always complicated. For Annuscha, writing is more than storytelling, it is discovery.
