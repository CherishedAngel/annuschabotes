Appearance

Slender, pale almost translucent skin, azure hair streaked white and black after the Shadow Temple trials, haunted blue eyes, ethereal grace, crescent scar near her left temple.

Inner Life

Personality: Reserved, passionate, compassionate, ruthlessly practical when needed, and afraid of abandonment.

Core motivation: Protect the innocent, uncover her origins, and choose her own fate.

Voice & Bonds

Speech style: Careful in public, fluid and passionate in private; her voice hints at power when angered.

Relationships: Cain sees her shadows; Garrin offers forbidden light; Mia challenges her; Ravan steadies her.
