Chapter Preview

A night of stillness

Moonlight bled through Entymora's canopy like liquid silver, pooling in hollows where bioluminescent flowers pulsed with the rhythm of sleeping hearts. The forest breathed—slow, ancient, eternal—carrying the scent of centuries in its exhale. Beneath its protective embrace, Fyrenshade dreamed, safe as a child tucked beneath its mother's wing. Above, Lunaria's fractured moon traced skeletal patterns across the landscape, casting shadows that whispered secrets only the wind could hear. Legend said the moon had shattered in the war against the Gluttonous One, when the ancient guardians fought to save Xyphara from consumption. Now its broken pieces hung in the sky like a reminder—some wounds never fully heal, some scars remain visible forever.
